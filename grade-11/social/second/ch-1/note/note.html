1. Introduction to Ecology

What is Ecology?

Ecology is the study of how organisms interact with one another and their environment.

Energy Flow

Energy enters an ecosystem as sunlight. Plants (producers) capture this energy, which then moves through consumers.

Food Chains

A food chain shows the linear flow of energy from producers to consumers.

[Image of a simple food chain]

Biotic & Abiotic Factors

Biotic factors are living; Abiotic factors are non-living like sunlight, temperature, and water.
