Help & Support

Find answers to common questions about BNA Academy

How do I access my courses?
From your dashboard, click on any subject card to access notes, quizzes, and video lessons for that subject.
How is my average calculated?
Your average is calculated from all assessments that teachers mark as "Include in average" including classwork, homework, quizzes, assignments, and final exams.
How do I message my teacher?
Go to Messages from the dropdown menu. You'll see your teachers listed there. Click on their name to start a conversation.
What do attendance marks mean?
P = Present (full access), A = Absent (need confirmation), L = Late (access granted), PER = Permission (pending admin approval).
I forgot my password
Click "Forgot Password" on the sign-in page. You'll receive an email with instructions to reset your password.
