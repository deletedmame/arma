Unit 5: Human Health, Nutrition and Diseases

5.1 Nutrients

Macronutrients: Carbohydrates (energy), Proteins (growth/repair), Fats (energy storage). Micronutrients: Vitamins (A, B, C, D, E, K) and Minerals (calcium, iron, iodine).

5.2 Balanced Diet

A balanced diet contains all nutrients in correct proportions based on age, sex, and activity level.

5.3 Deficiency Diseases

  • Kwashiorkor (protein deficiency) - swollen belly
  • Marasmus (energy deficiency) - extreme thinness
  • Anemia (iron) - weakness, tiredness
  • Rickets (Vitamin D) - soft bones
  • Scurvy (Vitamin C) - bleeding gums

5.4 Malnutrition

  • HIV/AIDS: Virus attacks immune system
  • Tuberculosis: Bacterial lung infection
  • Malaria: Plasmodium protozoa from mosquitoes
  • COVID-19: Coronavirus, respiratory droplets
Unit Summary: Nutrients include carbs, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals. Balanced diet prevents deficiency diseases. Malnutrition includes undernutrition and obesity. Substance abuse harms health. Infectious diseases are caused by pathogens.
